**Birds** (2026). Generative MIDI sequencer. The melody is drawn as birds on wires, and it drifts from the original within a limit you set. [Buy on Gumroad](https://aylesim.gumroad.com/l/birds) [needs: manual PDF link] [Watch video](/instruments/birds)

**Knob Studio** (2024). Modulation device. Layered functions form one waveform, and reading heads send its values to any mapped parameter. [Buy on Gumroad](https://aylesim.gumroad.com/l/knobstudio) [Watch video](/instruments/knob-studio)

**Disco** [needs year; site says 2022]. Multi-tap delay with per-tap gain, pan and on/off in a circular layout. The taps rotate together or separately, by hand or by LFO. [Buy on Gumroad](https://aylesim.gumroad.com/l/disco) [Watch video](/instruments/disco)

**Freesound4Live** (2020). Search and load samples from Freesound.org inside Ableton Live, with Freesound account login. Covered by Create Digital Music. [needs: current store link; whether it is open source] [Watch video](/instruments/freesound4live)

**Lucky Notes** (free) [needs year]. MIDI effect that lets notes through by probability, so a pattern can start sparse and get denser without editing clips. Listed in the ELPHNT Max for Live Curated Collection. [Download free](https://aylesim.gumroad.com/l/luckynotes)
