I trained in electronic music and I write the code for everything I make. Since 2018 I have released Max for Live devices under the name Aylesim Devices, from the interface to the manual and support. I have shown installations that turn live camera feeds, voices and Wikipedia edits into sound and image. For the web I build frontends in React, TypeScript and Next.js, often from a design team's Figma files, such as the North Sea chapter of the Berggruen Institute's Planetary Compendium.


Open to employment in Berlin or remote, including fixed-term contracts.
