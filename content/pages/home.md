I'm Alessandro, an Italian developer living in Berlin with a background in electronic music. I make software you can play music with, and websites like this one.

My instruments go by Aylesim Devices. They run inside Ableton Live. I design and code them myself.

Other projects take me outside the music setup: installations that turn Wikipedia edits into sound, or live camera feeds into visuals. You can find those in [Art](/art), and the music tools in [Instruments](/instruments).

I also organise the [Max Berlin Network](https://maxberlin.network), where we meet to share patches and help each other get things working.
