## Artist statement
My work is about surveillance and data: cameras left open online, edits to Wikipedia, voices fed through neural networks. I build software that runs live in the room: it pulls in material from networks, cameras or recordings and turns it into sound and image while the audience watches.

In Please Set a Password, live feeds from surveillance cameras left open on the internet are projected as they are found, and the sound is synthesised from what the images contain. There Will Be No More Determination uses a neural network trained on military helicopter footage, so the output still reads as aerial war vision but no longer shows a scene. Wikyslavery inserts misleading edits into Wikipedia and makes visible the unpaid work of the people who correct them. Fonema passes recorded voices through a neural network until they still sound like speech but stop meaning anything.

I studied electronic music at the Conservatorio Niccolò Piccinni in Bari. The same tools I use for these pieces, Max/MSP and real-time synthesis, are the ones I use to build instruments for Ableton Live.

## Biography
Alessandro Miracapillo is an Italian artist and software developer based in Berlin. He studied electronic music at the Conservatorio Niccolò Piccinni in Bari. His installations use live network data, surveillance feeds and neural networks to make sound and image in real time. Please Set a Password won first prize in Electronic Art at the 15th Premio Nazionale delle Arti (Italian Ministry of University and Research) in 2021. He developed work at the Con.divisione residencies at Maul in 2022 and 2023 and performed live visuals at TEDx Barletta in 2020. Since 2018 he has made Max for Live instruments under the name Aylesim Devices. In 2025 he founded the Max Berlin Network.
