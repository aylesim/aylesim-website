I'm Alessandro, an Italian developer living in Berlin. I make instruments for Ableton Live and build websites. Sometimes the code ends up in an installation, turning a camera feed or a Wikipedia edit into sound and image.

I studied electronic music in Bari. Since 2018 I've been releasing my own Max for Live instruments as Aylesim Devices. I design the interfaces and write the code, then help people use them once they're out in the world.

On the web, I work with designers to turn their layouts into sites people can use. One of those collaborations was the North Sea chapter of the Berggruen Institute's Planetary Compendium.

## Community

I started the [Max Berlin Network](https://maxberlin.network) so people using Max/MSP could meet in person. We share what we're making and work through patches that aren't behaving.

## Teaching

I've taught people to build websites at Fondazione Cascina Cotica, with everyone finishing the course with a site online. At La Scuola Open Source in Bari, I've run workshops on generative music and building things with sensors and Arduino.
